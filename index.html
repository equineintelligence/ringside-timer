Privacy

To sign in, we store your email address and a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password with our service provider, Supabase. We use your email only to let you sign in and reset your password; we don't share or sell it.

Your processed shows and starred riders are saved in your account, visible only to you, until you delete them. Live shows that organisers publish (with riders' and horses' names) are stored online so everyone can follow them, and are deleted automatically 7 days after the show. If you use "Read schedule with AI", the file is sent to an AI service (outside South Africa) to be processed and is not stored.

Privacy

Your account stores only your email address and a hashed password. Uploaded schedule files are never stored anywhere. Your processed shows and starred riders and horses are saved in your account (with a copy on this phone for offline use) until you delete them (above); only you can see them. Live shows you publish are stored online (with riders' and horses' names) and deleted automatically 7 days after the show date; only you and your marshals can change them. If you use "Read schedule with AI", the file is sent to an AI service (outside South Africa) to be processed and is not stored; we keep only an anonymous count of AI reads to prevent abuse. The free on-phone reader never sends your file anywhere.
